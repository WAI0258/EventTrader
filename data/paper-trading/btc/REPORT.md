# Bitcoin / BTC Perpetual Investment Decision Report

**Review interval: September 11, 2026, 22:20 UTC to September 15, 2026, 20:20 UTC**

## Executive summary

Research moved from neutral to briefly constructive, then weakened as flow conditions and catalysts changed. The paper portfolio retained the +0.5 long established before this interval and did not execute an exit.

PM returned **−0.81%**, with a **2.81% maximum drawdown**. Direct, the counterfactual mapping of research assessments to positions, returned **+1.14%**, with a **0.95% maximum drawdown**. This is a negative case for the portfolio: research responded to changing conditions, but the existing long did not transition into an executed risk exit.

At the cutoff, the latest research assessment was neutral while the paper portfolio remained long at +0.5. A neutral research assessment must not be described as a completed close, and an unexecuted exit condition must not be counted as realized risk reduction.

This report covers the full available overlapping valuation interval for the published BTC sample. It is shorter than 30 days; no selected 30-day case is presented.

## Decision evolution

### At the interval's start: manage an existing position rather than assume a fresh purchase

The earlier long was established on ETF-demand and rebound evidence. The portfolio already held +0.5 exposure when this report's valuation interval began.

PM therefore managed historical exposure, while Direct translated assessments updated during the interval into hypothetical positions. Their starting economic contexts were different, even though their displayed values were normalized to the same interval.

### September 12: flows remain a headwind and research stays neutral

The recorded ETF evidence indicated that outflows had slowed relative to preceding sessions. That was insufficient to establish a stable expansion in institutional demand.

Research did not interpret smaller outflows as a fresh bullish confirmation. Price was near the lower part of the recent range after a decline, without sufficiently clear reversal evidence. The assessment remained neutral.

No new execution changed the existing long.

### September 14: a regulatory catalyst briefly improves the research view

Recorded legislative developments improved the assessment of a near-term regulatory catalyst, while price rebounded from around **76,346**. Research briefly returned to weak long as the catalyst and recovery structure became more aligned.

ETF-outflow risk remained. Subsequent general market commentary did not supply additional flow or price confirmation, and research returned to neutral.

The portfolio did not add exposure, avoiding an increase in risk based solely on a temporary improvement in the catalyst assessment.

### September 15: the rebound fails, the catalyst weakens, and the existing long faces pressure

Price retreated from around **79,576**, and research alternated between weak short and neutral. Recorded legislative setbacks weakened a near-term catalyst that had supported the earlier long thesis.

The disappearance of a bullish catalyst was not treated as an automatic instruction to chase a short. The final assessment was neutral pending clearer structure, and it did not interpret the decline as a reason to add to the long.

At the cutoff, the research reference was approximately **75,894**, below **76,346** and within the area below **76,000**. PM continued to require a sustained invalidation confirmation and retained +0.5 exposure. No close executed.

The relevant question was therefore whether an already-pressured risk boundary would translate into timely portfolio action, rather than whether another narrative could justify continuing to hold.

## Interpretation of the result

Direct ended positive while PM lost money and experienced a larger drawdown. The research response to changing conditions was not fully transmitted into portfolio operations.

This does not imply that every research update should immediately produce a trade. It does identify a case where weaker research and continued long exposure diverged. When price enters the invalidation area, the absence of an executed close cannot be presented as evidence that risk has already been controlled.

## Action framework at the cutoff

- **Existing long:** do not add; prioritize whether the required persistent invalidation condition has been met.
- **76,000–76,346:** monitor whether sustained weakness results in an executed reduction or close, rather than only a revised assessment.
- **A renewed constructive view:** require price recovery, improved flows, and restored catalyst support together.
- **79,576:** use the failed-rebound reference to assess whether upward price acceptance returns.
- **Neutral research:** indicates insufficient advantage for a fresh direction; it does not mean an existing long has become risk-free.
