All curves are rebased to 1 at the review start. PM and Direct use the saved numerical results; Buy & Hold uses a fully exposed numerical benchmark over matching valuation times. Its underlying entry-cost treatment is retained before rebasing. No raw OHLCV is embedded.

Phase and interval returns are ratios of saved valuations, not standalone entry/exit-price trade returns. Boundaries use the latest valuation at or before the operation; when it differs, the valuation time is shown. Reopening gaps can therefore be attributed to the following phase. Phase percentages compound rather than add. Same-time open/close phases are excluded from the win rate. An open phase is an unrealized review-window result.

Additional costs use target-weight calendar-time accrual and multiplicative turnover charges on in-window weight changes. Carried-in positions incur no new opening fee; no hypothetical exit fee is charged at the cutoff. This is a sensitivity model, not a reconstruction of realized financing costs. Buy & Hold is the fully exposed reference and is not charged these additional PM-only scenario costs.
